About Us

Starting a Movement, Building a Legacy

Youth for Change was founded in 2024 by six young Pakistanis who believe that real change starts at the grassroots level. We are not just an organization — we are a movement of youth, for youth, and by youth.

Led by President Touqeer Ahmad, our team brings together diverse skills and shared passion. From education to climate action, we are laying the foundation for sustainable development across Pakistan, one community at a time.

Our Mission

To empower Pakistani youth through education, skills, and civic engagement for sustainable national development.

Our Vision

A Pakistan where every young person has the tools, opportunities, and voice to shape their future.

Our Structure

Youth for Change operates with a flat, collaborative structure. The Executive Committee (President, VP, Secretary) provides strategic leadership, while the Core Team (Finance, Programs, Communications) drives execution. Every voice matters, every idea counts.

Our Focus

What We Stand For

As a new organization, we are building our foundation on these core pillars.

Education Access

Every child deserves quality education. We aim to establish tutoring centers, provide scholarships, and bridge the digital divide in underserved communities.

Skills & Employment

Youth unemployment is our challenge to solve. We will deliver vocational training, digital skills, and entrepreneurship support to create job creators.

Climate Action

Pakistan faces severe climate challenges. Our tree plantation drives, awareness campaigns, and sustainable practices will protect our future.
